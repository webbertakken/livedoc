# PDF documents

Follow the references below only as needed; never upfront.

Research for making a PDF a document in LiveDoc: opened, read, reviewed and really edited, by people together.
Researched November 2026. Every claim is tagged **verified** (how), **inferred** (from what) or **assumed**. The
specs that come of it live in docs/specs/.

- ./the-standard.md - when asking what a PDF is by its standards, or what "any PDF" is in the wild
- ./engine.md - when choosing or changing what reads, draws and writes a PDF: the spikes and their numbers
- ./dark-pages.md - when working on how pages are shown dark: the four designs and the contrast curve
- ./corpus-and-validators.md - when adding a test file, or asking what says a written PDF is a good one
