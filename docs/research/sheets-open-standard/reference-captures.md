# Reference captures

Follow the references below only as needed; never upfront.

The pictures of Google Sheets each parity matrix row is checked against (Phase 18 walks them row by row).

## What public pages give

- Google's help pages carry small, partial pictures of the menus and panels, often older than the product
  (verified: the pages linked from the [inventory](google-sheets-inventory.md) show cropped dialogs only).
- A spreadsheet published to the web shows the grid and sheet bar read-only, without the menus, toolbar or panels
  (assumed). Editing needs a signed-in Google account, which agents here do not hold and must not create.
