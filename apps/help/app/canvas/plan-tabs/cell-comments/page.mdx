A comment starts a conversation about a cell or a range, as in Google Sheets: everyone who comments is named, replies gather under it, and it can be resolved when it is done. Use a note when you only want a cell to carry some text.

## Adding a Comment

1. Click the cell, or select the range the comment is about.
2. Choose **Insert › Comment**, the **Insert Comment** button in the toolbar, right-click and choose **Comment**, or press **Ctrl+Alt+M** (**⌘⌥M** on a Mac).
3. Type the comment. Type **@** or **+** and a name to mention someone; tick **Assign to** to make it their action.
4. Choose **Comment**, or press **Ctrl+Enter**. **Cancel** or **Esc** drops it.

## Reading and Replying

A cell with an open comment shows a small orange triangle in its top-right corner. Rest the pointer on the cell for a moment to read the first comment, who wrote it and how many replies follow. Click the cell to open the whole conversation beside it, with a field to reply. A cell with both a note and a comment shows the comment's triangle, and resting on it shows the note first.

On an open conversation, **J** and **K** move to the next and previous comment on the sheet, **R** replies, **E** resolves it and **Esc** or **U** goes back to the cells. From the cells, **Ctrl+Alt+N** then **C** moves to the next comment and **Ctrl+Alt+P** then **C** to the previous one (**Ctrl+⌘** on a Mac).

## The Comments Panel

Press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A** (**⌘⌥⇧A** on a Mac), or the comment history button beside the spreadsheet's name, to list every comment of the spreadsheet. Each one is labelled by its sheet and cells, such as **Budget!B4:C9**. Narrow the list to comments for you, to open or resolved ones, or to one sheet. Choose a comment to go to its cells with the conversation open, or **Reopen** a resolved one. A comment whose row or column was deleted stays in the list, marked as such, until the deletion is undone.

An Inbox notification about a comment opens the spreadsheet at its cells, with the conversation open.

## Resolving, Editing and Deleting

- **Resolve** closes the conversation and takes its triangle away; an assigned one reads **Mark as done**.
- Edit or delete your own comments from the comment's buttons. Editors can delete anyone's.
- Deleting the first comment deletes the whole conversation, after asking.

Comments are kept beside the cells, so adding one is never a step you undo with **Ctrl+Z**. Copying cells or a sheet leaves the comments with the original.

## Who Can Do What

Editors and commenters add, reply to, react to and resolve comments. People who can only view the spreadsheet see no comments. A spreadsheet kept in This Browser has no comments: sync it first.
