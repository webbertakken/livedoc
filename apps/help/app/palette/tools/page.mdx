There is no longer a **Tools** tab. Every group it held is now a palette **category** of its own, so this page is a map of where each tool went.

The categories come in groups: **Common**, **Structure**, **Decorate** and **Dynamic**, plus the session kit while the Workshop control is on. In Structured mode they are the bands of the strip's category picker, which opens on [Popular](/help/palette/popular/); in Pages mode and with the Workshop control on they sit along the strip by name, a thin line between each group: rest on one, or click it, to see its elements.

## Which categories each mode offers

The palette is laid out per editor mode, so not every category appears everywhere:

- **Structured** (the default): Popular (where the strip opens), Shapes, Write (text, notes, the Comment panel and the Action card), Draw (the pens and arrows), Build as two categories, **Diagrams** (mind nodes, org charts, flowcharts, sequence diagrams, tables, entities) and **Layout** (lanes, frames, stacks, boundaries, the diagram key, cards, badges and step numbers), after Concepts (with the Sankey and the Timeline), Icons, Tech and Media (Image and Avatar). **Library** (your shape libraries) joins Common once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/), and **Event Storming** joins Structure on an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/).
- **[Pages](/help/canvas/pages-mode/)**: Shapes, Write, Concepts (with the Sankey), Components (ending with the Stack), Devices, Icons, Stickers, Media (with the embeds), and Data (ending with the Table). The Timeline is in Concepts. There is no Draw, Build or Tech, and no Collaborate until the Workshop control is on, and Write leaves out the diagram-only tiles (Page and Annotation). **Logo** leads Common while the tab has a logo page, and **Library** joins once you have imported a shape library.
- **The [Workshop control](/help/canvas/workshop/)**, on, adds the session kit to Structured or Pages: **Ask**, and **Session**, which holds Tools, Record, React, Selection Mode and Navigate, before Icons, and **Stickers** after Icons in Structured. Every category the mode had stays.
- **[Plan tabs](/help/canvas/plan-tabs/)**: no palette. Boards, sheets and dashboards come from **Start Planning**, cards from a column's **Add card**, widgets from the **+** in a board's header, and metrics and charts from a dashboard's **Add to Slot**.
- **[Sketch mode](/help/canvas/sketch-mode/)** swaps the palette for the whiteboard's own pens and shapes.

Anything a mode leaves out is still one search away: the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) can add any palette element by name, whatever the mode.

## Where each tool lives

**Build** (in Structured mode, **Diagrams** and **Layout**) holds the elements you lay a diagram out with, the ones that hold other work: **Mind Node** (Tab adds a child, Enter a sibling), **Table** (an editable grid of cells), **Lane** (a titled band that carries its steps) and **Frame** (a labelled box that groups a section, `F`). The **Timeline** is a [concept graphic](/help/palette/concept-graphics/timeline-rail/) now, in **Concepts**.

**Write** holds the wordy elements: **Page** is a paper-sized sheet you write into, **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`), **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`), and **Annotation** is a small marker that holds a note. The coloured Event Storming workshop stickies live in [their own category](/help/palette/event-storming/), which appears on an Event Storming board.

**Draw** holds the gesture tools: **Freehand** (`P`) sketches a stroke and keeps it exactly as drawn, the **Shape Pen** (`6`) turns a rough sketch into a real shape, the **Highlighter** lays one wide translucent marker stroke, **Polygon** places straight-edged outlines point by point, **Arrow** (`A`) drops a connector with a pointer at its end that you position by hand, and **Line** drops the same without the pointer. See [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) and [Highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/).

**Components** (Pages) holds the ready-made pieces in three groups: **Layout** (Hero, Header, Banner and the Stack; Hero needs image uploads, like Image and Avatar), **Page Blocks** (Card, Badge, Step Number, Callout, Stat Row, Process) and **Code and Lists** (Code, Checklist). Entity is a Structured element. In Structured mode, **Entity** is in Build; add a **Code block** or a **Checklist** from the search panel.

**Data** (Pages) holds the charts and meters: Pie, Bar, Line, Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating. See [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/).

**Collaborate** (with the Workshop control on) is the elements that do something when somebody interacts with them, or that ask the room something, in six groups: **Ask** on the strip (dot vote, poll, quiz, estimate card, temperature check, idea box, Q&A board), and inside **Session**: **Tools** (timer, stopwatch, reveal zone, done check, picker), **Record** (comment panel, action panel, agenda, decision record, roll call), **React** (the five reaction pads), **Selection Mode** buttons, and **Navigate** (portal, bring focus, chair, desk, sofa, fence, link card, and the virtual office: room, meeting table, plant, bookshelf, water cooler, coffee machine, rug). See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) and [Collaborate Elements](/help/palette/collaborate/).

**Media** covers pictures and other people's pages: **Image** (`9`) and **Avatar**, plus, in Pages, an **Embed** group for YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs and any website ([embeds](/help/palette/embed-elements/)). Image and Avatar only appear where image uploads are available.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select it, restyle it, multi-select it with others, and move it freely. Pick a tile and tap the canvas to drop the element at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want.

## Guides for these tools

Several tools have their own walkthroughs, listed below and linked here:

- [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) covers Freehand, the Shape Pen, and the Polygon tool.
- [Highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/) covers the translucent marker and its colours and widths.
- [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/) covers uploading, searching for and reusing pictures, and avatars.
- [Pages](/help/palette/tools/pages/) covers the paper-sized sheet for prose.
- [Tables](/help/palette/tools/tables/) covers the editable grid: adding one, filling cells, and rows/columns.
- [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) covers the coloured note card for short annotations.
- [Code Blocks](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) covers the monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets.
- [Checklists](/help/palette/tools/checklists/) covers the card of checkable to-do rows you tick on the canvas.
- [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/) covers linking two portals and travelling between them, including across tabs.
- [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/) covers the **Data** category: Pie, Column and Line charts (each any of eighteen chart types), Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating.
- [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) covers the **Collaborate** categories' interactive elements: Selection Mode buttons, Reveal zones, Done checks, Pickers, Bring Focus, the Timer and Stopwatch, and Reaction pads.

Arrows are deep enough to deserve their own guide: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
